# Hammond BIM Tech

**Civil Engineering · BIM · Digital Construction · Engineering Automation**

[Visit Website](https://hammondbimtech.com/)

Hammond BIM Tech is an independent engineering technology initiative exploring the intersection of civil engineering, Building Information Modelling (BIM) and software development.

The website is designed and developed in-house as both a professional portfolio and an evolving technical platform. Its purpose is to connect engineering knowledge with practical digital workflows, demonstrate technical projects, and explore better ways to model, manage and deliver engineering information.

## Website Development

The website is built from the ground up, with custom-developed frontend components and content systems rather than relying on a generic website template.

Current development includes:

- **Custom website design** — a Bauhaus-inspired visual identity influenced by engineering, architecture and technical drawing.
- **Dynamic blog system** — custom-coded article loading and rendering to publish technical writing and document ongoing research.
- **Technical knowledge base** — a foundation for organising engineering knowledge, software workflows and technical resources.
- **Portfolio and technical studies** — a platform for presenting projects, modelling studies and practical experiments.
- **Responsive frontend** — custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript designed for a consistent experience across devices.

## Engineering & Digital Construction

The platform reflects an asset-centred approach to digital engineering, connecting design information with construction delivery and, ultimately, asset operation.

Areas of interest include:

- **Civil & Structural Engineering** — infrastructure, structural modelling and engineering workflows.
- **BIM & Digital Construction** — Revit, model coordination, reinforcement detailing and digital delivery.
- **Engineering Automation** — Python-based tools and custom software for engineering tasks.
- **OpenBIM & Interoperability** — IFC, structured model data and connected workflows.
- **Digital Assets** — information management, IoT monitoring, smart buildings and digital twins.

## Planned Development

Hammond BIM Tech is intended to evolve beyond a conventional portfolio website into an interactive engineering resource.

Planned features include:

- **Interactive IFC viewers** — browser-based exploration of building and infrastructure models.
- **Technical knowledge base** — structured articles, guides and reference material covering engineering, BIM and software.
- **Knowledge-based video content** — demonstrations, tutorials and technical explainers.
- **Engineering automation projects** — practical tools for model data extraction, quantity analysis and workflow improvement.
- **Connected digital workflows** — exploring how engineering models and asset information can support construction and operational use.

These features will be developed progressively as the underlying tools, projects and content mature.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Hosting & Storage:** Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare R2
- **Engineering & BIM:** Autodesk Revit, Civil 3D, IFC and related digital engineering tools

## Project Philosophy

The aim is to combine practical engineering understanding with software development to create useful, accessible digital tools for the built environment.

Rather than treating BIM as modelling alone, Hammond BIM Tech explores how structured information, automation and connected technologies can support the asset lifecycle — from design and pre-construction through construction delivery and into operation.
