About

Connecting Engineering and Technology

With a background in civil engineering and construction experience in both the UK and Australia, I've worked across surveying, site engineering, setting out and design-related modelling.

I'm particularly interested in the growing role of BIM, engineering data, automation and software in the built environment. Through Hammond BIM Tech, I document my work, investigate technical challenges and develop practical tools and workflows, while offering engineering teams flexible support across modelling, digital delivery and programming.
