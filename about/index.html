My Approach

Engineering, Built Together.

I created Hammond BIM Tech to bring practical engineering and emerging technology closer together. I want to work alongside design and construction teams, contributing to real projects through engineering, BIM modelling and digital delivery while continuing to explore how technology can improve the way we work.

Australia · Major Engineering Projects

While travelling and working across Australia, I contributed to a range of major projects. Also seeing the data we were throwing away started me thinkinng there must have be a better way to do things.

STRUCTURES / BIM

Model Creation & Drafting

My initial focus is reinforced concrete structures, with particular attention to the accurate representation of reinforcement. I would welcome opportunities to support experienced design teams on a freelance basis while continuing to develop my understanding of design processes and best practice. I also want to help construction teams prepare for delivery through constructability-focused models and digital information that make requirements clearer before work begins, reducing avoidable delays and issues discovered on site.

Over time, I aim to extend this work into civil infrastructure, earthworks and drainage, bringing different engineering disciplines together in coordinated digital environments.
