THE NEXT CHAPTER

Bringing Engineering & Technology Together

Hammond BIM Tech

Hammond BIM Tech aims to provide flexible, cost-effective technical support to existing teams across design, estimating and construction. By combining BIM modelling, drafting, detailing, data analysis and custom software development, I want to help clients get more value from their models, improve the information available to project teams, and automate or streamline tasks that might otherwise be too labour-intensive or costly to undertake. I am also interested in client-side BIM, helping developers establish effective information requirements and make better use of digital models throughout the delivery process.

My long-term ambition is to build a multidisciplinary practice through collaboration with experienced engineers, designers, contractors and developers. By learning from the challenges faced across different disciplines, I hope to develop practical digital tools and workflows that address real project needs, with the eventual goal of supporting digital twins from initial concept and design through construction, operation and ongoing asset management.

Areas of Interest

Engineering, Data & Digital Construction

Hammond BIM Tech aims to provide flexible, cost-effective technical support to existing teams across pre-construction, construction delivery and asset management. By combining engineering knowledge, BIM, programming and data tools, I want to help clients get more value from their digital models, improve the quality of project information and develop practical solutions to challenges across the asset lifecycle.

01 PRE-CONSTRUCTION
MODELLING / ESTIMATING

Design, Modelling & Estimating

I'm interested in providing additional technical capacity to design teams during pre-construction, supporting the development of digital models, drawings and technical documentation. My initial focus is reinforced concrete structures, with an emphasis on accurate modelling, reinforcement detailing and producing reliable information from BIM.

I also want to explore how model-based quantities, custom software and automation can make estimating and other pre-construction activities more efficient. By streamlining repetitive or labour-intensive tasks, I aim to make useful digital workflows more accessible and cost-effective for engineering teams.

AREAS OF WORK
  • Structural BIM modelling
  • Revit modelling & detailing
  • Technical drawings & documentation
  • Model-based quantity take-offs
  • Estimating & quantity extraction
  • Custom scripts & automation
02 CONSTRUCTION DELIVERY
VALIDATION / CONSTRUCTABILITY

Coordination, Validation & Constructability

I'm interested in supporting construction teams by making design information easier to interpret, validate and use during delivery. This includes reviewing drawings against digital models, extracting quantities and developing workflows that help identify discrepancies and potential constructability issues before they affect work on site.

By combining practical engineering experience with BIM and programming, I want to develop tools that reduce manual checking, improve information quality and help bridge the gap between design intent and construction requirements. My aim is to provide targeted support where additional technical work can improve project delivery without adding unnecessary cost.

AREAS OF WORK
  • Drawing and model validation
  • Model-based quantity take-offs
  • Constructability reviews
  • Design coordination support
  • Automated checking & reporting
  • Digital construction workflows
03 CLIENT-SIDE BIM
BIM / IOT / DIGITAL TWINS

Information Management & Smart Buildings

I'm interested in how developers can get the greatest long-term value from BIM, from establishing project information requirements through to receiving structured, reliable asset data at handover. This means looking beyond the delivery of a coordinated model and considering how project information can support the operation, maintenance and management of an asset throughout its lifecycle.

I'm also interested in the potential of IoT monitoring, connected building systems and smart-building technologies to bring digital models closer to the real-world performance of an asset. By exploring how model data can connect with operational information, sensor readings and building management systems, I want to understand how digital twins can support monitoring, maintenance and better-informed decisions.

My long-term ambition is to work with developers, engineers and information management professionals to help connect design, construction and operation, making digital information useful long after a project is complete.

AREAS OF INTEREST
  • Client-side BIM strategy & support
  • Information requirements & deliverables
  • Model data quality & validation
  • Asset information & digital handover
  • IoT monitoring & connected systems
  • Smart buildings & building performance
  • Digital twins & lifecycle data
  • Facilities & asset management
