Project approach

This project was developed to demonstrate structural modelling and drafting capability within a more complex reinforced concrete arrangement. The design is intentionally simple in overall form, but incorporates a number of junctions between columns, beams and slabs that provide opportunities to demonstrate more detailed modelling and reinforcement detailing.

A particular focus of the project is the treatment of the cantilevered sections and how their reinforcement requirements differ from those of the surrounding beams. The project therefore provides an opportunity to demonstrate not only the physical modelling of the structure, but also consideration of how reinforcement should respond to changes in structural behaviour and geometry.

The model is for illustration purposes only rather than a full structural design. The objective is to demonstrate modelling, detailing and drafting capability while maintaining a coherent engineering rationale behind the structural arrangement. The project will be developed further over time, with additional reinforcement detailing and associated drawings.

Future development of the project may also extend beyond the structural model to include landscaping, civil works and a conceptual drainage arrangement. This will provide an opportunity to consider how the structural design interfaces with the surrounding site and how the different elements can be coordinated within a broader project model.
