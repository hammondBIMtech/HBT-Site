Project approach

This project draws on my experience working on LNG infrastructure in Australia, where I was involved in setting out structures of a similar nature. It forms part of a series of smaller projects intended to demonstrate my modelling and drafting abilities.

The model for illustration purposes only rather than a full structural design. The objective is to demonstrate modelling, detailing and drafting capability while maintaining a coherent engineering rationale behind the decisions shown. The project will be developed further over time, including reinforcement drawings.

The structural concept is intentionally straightforward. The tank foundation is formed as a deeper slab than the surrounding area, while the surrounding slab is graded simply to provide a practical and buildable arrangement. The drainage channel follows the same principle. Although the channel may initially appear more substantial than strictly necessary, the supporting walls are maintained at a consistent height rather than following the changing channel grade. This creates a simpler and more repeatable construction arrangement whilst providing uniform support for the load bearing grate.

The resulting foundation therefore uses a small number of straightforward slab break lines together with an integrated drainage channel to achieve the required geometry and functionality. One consideration that would require further assessment in a full design is the potential asymmetric loading introduced by the drainage channel and its effect on the foundation system.
